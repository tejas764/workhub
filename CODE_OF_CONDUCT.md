# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want WorkHub AI to be a welcoming place for contributors, researchers and maintainers. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions is expected to communicate respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Provide constructive, specific and considerate feedback
- Assume good intentions while asking for clarification
- Make space for contributors with different experience levels
- Respect privacy and confidentiality, especially around academic records
- Credit the work of collaborators and third-party authors

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, threats, publication of private information, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may ask participants to revise content, pause discussions, remove abusive comments, or restrict contributions where necessary to keep the project respectful.

This policy governs collaboration in this repository and does not supersede institution rules or applicable laws.
